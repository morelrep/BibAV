<div data-schema-version="9"><p>But we, hardly preserving a vestige of our former state, neither Indians nor Europeans, but a race between the original natives and the European Spaniards; being by birth Americans, and our rights those of Europe, we have to dispute and fight for these contending interests, and to persevere in our endeavours notwithstanding the opposition of our invaders; so that we are placed in a most extraordinary and embarrassing dilemma.</p> </div>
